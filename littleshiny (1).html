About LittleShiny

LittleShiny is a freelance marketplace built to connect businesses with vetted, talented freelancers across design, development, marketing, writing, video, music, business and AI services.

We believe great work happens when clients and freelancers can trust the platform they're using — that means real accounts, secure escrow payments, and clear communication every step of the way.

Whether you're hiring for your next big idea or building a freelance career on your own terms, LittleShiny is built for you.

4. Payments and escrow

Payments are processed by Stripe. Clients and Freelancers may open a dispute if something goes wrong; LittleShiny's moderators decide the outcome of disputed orders at their reasonable discretion.

LittleShiny is not a bank and does not guarantee any particular outcome of a dispute. Chargebacks, fraud, and payment processor holds are governed by Stripe's own terms in addition to these Terms.

To operate the marketplace: matching freelancers to jobs, processing payments, sending notifications, showing public profiles, and moderating reports/disputes.
